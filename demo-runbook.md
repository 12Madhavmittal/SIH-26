# Annadata Direct — Guided Live Demo

## Opening: the consumer-affairs gap

Start on the home page and state the problem in one sentence: **India’s fresh-produce chain often hides both the farmer’s earnings and the buyer’s final cost. Annadata Direct makes a direct, traceable, demand-aware path visible.** Point to the statement that the prototype serves consumers, bulk buyers, farmers/FPOs, and logistics partners in one connected operating model.

## Step 1: discover a direct, source-referenced lot

Open the marketplace and select **Sun-ripened tomato**. Explain that the dated mandi reference is drawn from the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare’s daily mandi-price dataset, while the direct offer is an illustrative FPO-coordinated grade-A lot. The direct-price panel answers two questions a normal marketplace does not: **what the buyer pays** and **what the farmer earns**.

## Step 2: make the price chain accountable

Show the transparent price breakdown. Emphasise that the direct buyer price openly allocates farmer earnings, FPO services, quality and packing, and logistics. Then compare it with the illustrative conventional chain: the buyer saves money while the farmer’s per-kilogram share rises. The insight is not simply “cheaper”; it is **more explainable and more equitable**.

## Step 3: prove that FPO aggregation does not erase the farmer

Open the traceability panel for the tomato lot. The FPO aggregates a buyer-ready 620 kg lot from three small-farmer contributions, but it preserves a farmer code, harvest cluster, quantity, grade, and harvest date for each contribution. This enables settlement, quality accountability, recall readiness, and farmer-level impact measurement without exposing personal data.

## Step 4: place and consolidate an order

Reserve a consumer or bulk demonstration order. The confirmation identifies the correct delivery wave rather than treating every order as an individual trip. This is where the product connects marketplace demand to shared logistics.

## Step 5: show explainable demand intelligence

Open the operations page. Use the tomato demand card to show how recent direct orders, a confirmed bulk commitment, and the direction of market arrivals result in a forecast, a recommended listing quantity, a supply buffer, and a risk indicator. The methodology is visibly described so that an FPO can challenge and trust the recommendation.

## Step 6: turn multiple deliveries into one efficient route

Highlight the South Chennai delivery wave. The system displays vehicle capacity, planned load, grouped stops, baseline distance, optimized distance, estimated cost saving, and estimated emission saving. This directly answers the logistics requirement of PS 26033.

## Closing: measure what changes

Open the command center. Summarise the dashboard in one line: **Annadata Direct measures fulfilled orders, direct-trade value, farmer-income uplift, buyer savings, avoidable waste, route efficiency, and on-time delivery in one operating view.** End by noting that the source-backed pricing and fully explainable demo models are designed for a practical pilot with FPOs and institutional buyers.

## Validation note

The product-detail interaction has been tested on the Sun-ripened tomato lot. It opens the full transparent price allocation, dated AGMARKNET/data.gov.in mandi reference, three farmer-level contribution records, and a working consumer/bulk demo-reservation action. The reservation response confirms its destination delivery wave.

The FPO studio has also been visually verified. It exposes a demo listing form, a three-farmer contribution table, a calculated buyer-ready total, and server-backed actions that persist the clearly labelled demonstration records. The production equivalents are protected for authenticated FPO or administrator roles.

The FPO listing save action was executed successfully in the browser and returned a persisted listing identifier. This confirms that the studio’s demonstration action uses the database-backed server workflow rather than a client-only mock interaction.

The lot-assembly action was also executed successfully. It produced a stored lot code and confirmed all three farmer contribution records were retained in the persisted buyer-ready lot.
